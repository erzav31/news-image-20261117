## The day, in six stories

1. **Kerch Bridge hit again (lead)** — a road span collapses after a second
night of Ukrainian strikes on the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea;
Moscow vows to rebuild.
2. **Tankers hit by mines off Hormuz** — two oil tankers catch fire on a route
Iran calls unauthorised.
3. **Trump, Xi and Putin to meet Thursday** — China confirms a first three-way
summit in Shenzhen, at the APEC leaders' meeting.
4. **Chase outage: software bug** — online banking and Zelle slow for hours;
the bank says it was not a cyberattack.
5. **Vulcan fails — 40 satellites lost** — ULA's Vulcan fails minutes after
launch, destroying Amazon's 40 Leo internet satellites.
6. **Five nations plan Rwanda return hub** — Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece
and the Netherlands back a joint return facility for rejected asylum
seekers.
7. **Nvidia reports after the close** — first earnings since Jensen Huang
stepped down as chief executive.

Also: Russia says it intercepted 1,336 Ukrainian drones in a single day; CNN
reports US intelligence on an alleged Iran dirty-bomb plot — the White House
says no credible threat.

Sources: BBC, AP, Reuters, Al Jazeera, CNN, ULA, Spaceflight Now — as
reported on 17 November 2026.
